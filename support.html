Blorb Support

Official help for the Blorb virtual-pet and island adventure on iPhone.

Contact the studio

Email liquiditystudio2@gmail.com and describe your issue, your iPhone model, iOS version, and Blorb app version. Please do not send passwords, payment details or private photographs.

Install the iPhone beta

Download Apple TestFlight, then visit the official Blorb invitation. External access requires Apple beta approval and an available tester slot.

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the game and check your internet connection.
  • Camera and photo access are optional. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.
  • Some game progress is stored on your device. Avoid deleting the app unless you are prepared to lose locally stored progress.

Ads, purchases and refunds

Rewarded ads are optional. Purchases, when available, are processed by Apple and are not charged during TestFlight sandbox testing. Select Restore Apple Purchases in Blorb for eligible purchases. For App Store charges or refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Feedback

To report a crash, lost progress, missing reward or purchase problem, include the steps to reproduce it and the time it occurred. Our contact address is liquiditystudio2@gmail.com.
