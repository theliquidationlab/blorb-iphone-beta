Privacy Policy

How Blorb handles gameplay data, optional camera access, rewarded advertising, purchases and support.

Effective November 14, 2026

Developer and contact

Blorb is published by Liquidity Labs Studio. Contact liquiditystudio2@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests regarding support correspondence.

Gameplay information

Blorb stores your virtual pet, progress, owned cosmetics, rewards and preferences in on-device app storage. Removing the app may remove this data. You can play without sending a support email or providing payment details.

Camera and photos

Snack Cam and image-selection features may request access to the camera or photo library only when selected. You may decline or revoke these permissions in iPhone Settings. We do not use photographs for targeted advertising.

Optional advertising

Blorb can display opt-in rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. TestFlight may show demonstration ads. When production advertising is enabled, Google's SDK may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, subject to applicable consent and permissions. See Google's privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Apple in-app purchases

Apple processes any in-app payments. Blorb may receive product IDs and verified transaction records to deliver or restore purchased virtual items; we do not receive complete credit-card information. TestFlight purchases run in Apple's sandbox and are not charged.

Support correspondence

If you voluntarily contact us, we receive your email address and the details you send so we can help. We keep correspondence only as reasonably needed for support, legal obligations or fraud prevention.

Your choices and retention

You may choose not to use Snack Cam or optional ads, adjust iOS app permissions, and contact us about support data. Game progress stored on-device follows the app's storage lifecycle. Platform providers may handle additional information under their policies.

Changes

We may revise this policy when game features, hosting or service providers change. Material changes will be reflected here and in applicable App Store privacy disclosures before release.
