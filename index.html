Questions

Before you bounce.

Is the beta free?

Yes. TestFlight is free to download and Blorb beta access is free. Apple processes beta installations.

What if Apple says the beta isn't available?

Apple must approve the build for external beta testing, and the developer can limit invites. If access isn't available yet, check back after Apple enables the group or email the studio.

Can I play Blorb in this website?

The bouncing character above is an interactive mini preview. The actual Blorb game runs on a compatible iPhone or iPad after you install it through Apple's TestFlight.

Where do I report bugs?

Use Apple's TestFlight feedback controls or send a message to liquiditystudio2@gmail.com.
