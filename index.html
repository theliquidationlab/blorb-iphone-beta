A few quick answers

Before you play.

Is the beta free?

Yes, the beta and Apple's TestFlight app are free.

What if the invitation doesn't let me install the game?

Apple must approve the build for external beta testing. Availability also depends on supported devices and available tester slots. The page may be shared before external testing opens.

Is the website a playable demo?

No. This page displays official Blorb artwork, not a simulation of gameplay. The game itself is installed through Apple's TestFlight app.

How do I submit a bug?

Use the feedback option in TestFlight, or email liquiditystudio2@gmail.com.
